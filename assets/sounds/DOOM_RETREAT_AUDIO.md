# Doom retreat sound

`doom_retreat.wav` is an original synthesized winding-back and rising bell cue,
generated by `tools/generate_doom_retreat_sound.py`. It contains no third-party
samples. One cue plays at each retreating clock step; initial setup, unchanged
values and fast-forward remain silent, matching the advance cue's behavior.
Playback now uses the preloaded `GameplaySounds.Cue.DOOM_RETREAT` at 0.90 volume
(previously 0.40, about +7 dB). A genuine retreat from zero sounds as well;
initialization, HUD refreshes and awakening resets use the separate silent path.
