# Azathoth ending sound

`azathoth_ending.wav` is a three-second mono trim of “Stingers and Stabs 001” by OverlookHotelRecords, downloaded from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/OverlookHotelRecords/sounds/271945/). The source is released under CC0, so it can be copied, modified, and distributed commercially without attribution. The trim keeps the dramatic horror impact and fades its tail for the ending dialog.

The game uses 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM and plays the cue once when the dialog opens, stopping it when the dialog is dismissed.
