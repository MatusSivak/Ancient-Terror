# Doom advance sound

`doom_advance.wav` is the final chime strike (3 s, mono) from “German Grandfather Clock Tick & Chime x12” by 3bagbrew, downloaded from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/3bagbrew/sounds/609763/). The source is released under CC0, so it can be copied, modified, and distributed commercially without attribution. The original preview is kept in `source_assets/sounds/doom_advance/`; `tools/generate_doom_advance_sound.py` cuts the strike, fades its tail and normalizes it.

The doom clock tolls once per space the doom advances, at the start of each hand step. It is silent during the initial setup and in fast-forward.
