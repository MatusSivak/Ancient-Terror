Ancient gold and patinated jade ornaments integrated into the creature, with restrained intricate details. Keep the face, scales and silhouette legible when reduced to a small game icon.
Composition: entire creature and entire headdress visible, centered and filling about 88–92 percent of the square, comfortable transparent margins on every edge, nothing cropped. Coils form a compact base. Rich sculptural texture, convincing mass, crisp organic contour, dramatic restrained lighting matching references.
No scenery, temples, landscape, sky, ground plane, pedestal, border, text, logo, watermark, extra creatures, wings, limbs, or rectangular shadow. No painted black/white background or checkerboard pattern. Outside the isolated creature must be actual transparent pixels. Output exactly 1024x1024.
