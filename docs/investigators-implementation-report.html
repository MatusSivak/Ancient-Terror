Engine gaps

Missing systems: Unique Assets (no card type, although the RELIC/TASK traits exist), Talent conditions (Composed, Rugged, Elusive, Headstrong, Quick Study), Resource tokens, Impairment tokens, Wanted and Corruption conditions. (Uncharted paths are walk paths, which the game already has.)

Missing starting cards: Winchester Rifle, .25 Automatic, Fine Jewelry, .32 Colt Pocket, Tommy Gun, Magnifying Glass, Camera, Seek the Truth, Dream Box, Dream Diary, Ancient Tome, Sledgehammer, Investment, Lucky Talisman, Treasured Memento, Mauser C96, Arcane Assistant and Alchemical Concoction (assets), plus the spells Call the Storm, Binding and Find Gate.

Already supported: focus, travel tickets, Blessed/Cursed, Madness, Debt/Detained, reserve operations, "1 additional action" effects, and the Trade, Rest, Acquire Assets and ticket actions.

Recommendation

The first four are implemented. Continue with the Tier 2 group. Adding Unique Asset support unlocks 10 more investigators, so it's the most valuable engine feature. Talents come next: they would finish Vincent and Wendy fully and unlock Rita, plus Amanda and Daniela once Resources and Impairment exist.
